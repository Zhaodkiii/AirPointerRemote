# GlideCue Remote

Control your Mac from an iPhone or Apple Watch. Use your iPhone as a trackpad or air mouse, or view and control your Mac desktop over your local network.

[Watch the 18-second demo](docs/glidecue-demo.mp4)

## Quick Start

Open the receiver on your Mac and grant Bluetooth and Accessibility access. On your iPhone or Apple Watch, select your Mac and confirm the pairing. To view your Mac desktop on iPhone, connect both devices to the same local network.

**Requirements** · iOS 18+, watchOS 11+, macOS 14+
