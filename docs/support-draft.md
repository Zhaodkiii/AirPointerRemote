# AirPointer Remote Support

## Get connected

1. Open AirPointer Remote on your Mac and locate its pointer icon in the menu bar. Turn on Bluetooth and allow the app in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility**.
2. Open AirPointer Remote on your iPhone or Apple Watch and choose the Mac. On iPhone, make sure both devices show the same pairing code before approving. On Apple Watch, approve the connection on the Mac.
3. On iPhone, swipe in Trackpad mode or hold to move in Air Mouse mode. On Apple Watch, turn your wrist to move, tap the pad to click, or turn the Digital Crown to scroll.

If the Mac does not appear, bring the devices closer, check Bluetooth, and make sure the Mac app is running. If the devices are connected but the pointer does not move, check Mac Accessibility permission and whether control is paused. If another device has control, disconnect it first.

To stop control, choose **Stop All Control** from the Mac menu or press **Control + Option + Command + Period**. Resume control from the Mac menu when ready.

## View the Mac display on iPhone

Pair over Bluetooth, connect both devices to a local network, and tap the Linked Screen icon on iPhone. Tap **Start Sharing** and select a display in the Mac system picker. During sharing, choose **Remember “display” for iPhone** from the Mac menu bar to reuse that display for the same paired phone. Allow Screen Recording on the Mac when prompted. If the display is unavailable, choose a display again. To reset this preference, choose **Forget All Remembered Displays**. Grant Local Network access when prompted. If discovery times out, check both devices’ local network permissions and whether the Wi-Fi network isolates clients.

Tap to click, hold for a right click, or enable Drag and slide. In Direct mode, pinch to zoom and pan with two fingers. In Trackpad mode, two fingers scroll the Mac. Click an editable Mac field, type on iPhone and tap **Insert**. Password fields and unsupported editors do not accept linked text. Use the target app’s own Send or Save control afterward.

**Keep Mac Awake** applies during a sharing session. Closed-lid use depends on the Mac having an active external display and a supported power and input setup. Enable Keep Mac Awake before starting and select the external display. The app can continue when closing the lid moves that display to a new desktop position. If the selected display disappears, changes dimensions or sleeps, select a display again.
