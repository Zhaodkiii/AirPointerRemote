# AirPointer Remote Privacy Policy

AirPointer Remote connects a nearby iPhone or Apple Watch to a Mac you control. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or a developer-operated service that receives pointer input.

The app uses Bluetooth to discover and connect to your Mac. It sends touch and motion input directly to the connected Mac to move the pointer, click, drag, or scroll. Apple Watch sends its device name, model, system version, and battery status to the Mac to display connection information. The Mac needs Accessibility permission to send these pointer events to macOS.

Linked Screen sends the display you choose in the Mac system picker to your paired iPhone over an encrypted local network connection. If you enable Remember Display on the Mac, it stores the selected display identifier locally for that paired iPhone and can share it again on request. You can clear this choice from the Mac menu bar. Mac checks the focused control’s type and editability to show the iPhone keyboard. Draft text stays on iPhone until you tap Insert. Choosing Edit Selected Mac Text requests the selected passage for editing on iPhone. Replace and Delete check that the same field and text are still selected. Confirming a formatted paste replaces the Mac clipboard with the submitted formatted text. The app does not record audio, save video or submitted text, or send them to the publisher.

The app stores settings and pairing information on your devices so you can use them again. To remove a pairing, use **Forget Paired Mac** on iPhone, **Forget** on Apple Watch, or **Revoke All iPhone Pairings** from the Mac menu bar. You can revoke Bluetooth access in system settings and revoke pointer control in Mac Accessibility settings. Local diagnostic messages may be visible on your Mac.
