# AirPointer Remote Privacy Policy

AirPointer Remote connects a nearby iPhone or Apple Watch to a Mac you control. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or a developer-operated service that receives pointer input.

The app uses Bluetooth to discover and connect to your Mac. It sends touch and motion input directly to the connected Mac to move the pointer, click, drag, or scroll. Apple Watch sends its device name, model, system version, and battery status to the Mac to display connection information. The Mac needs Accessibility permission to send these pointer events to macOS. The app does not use this permission to read the contents of other apps.

The app stores settings and pairing information on your devices so you can use them again. To remove a pairing, use **Forget Paired Mac** on iPhone, **Forget** on Apple Watch, or **Revoke All iPhone Pairings** from the Mac menu bar. You can revoke Bluetooth access in system settings and revoke pointer control in Mac Accessibility settings. Local diagnostic messages may be visible on your Mac.
